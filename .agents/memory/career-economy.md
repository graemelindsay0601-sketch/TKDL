Review the Career economy across all routes, not only tournament prize money or sponsorships. A player should be able to focus on streaming and make a viable living without pursuing professional tour progression.

Living costs must never be forced. Earnings should fund optional lifestyle and progression purchases; DartCast studio investments are one-off expenses that improve future audience reach, not darts ability.

**Why:** the user said the economy and sponsors may be weak across Career and that streamers need an independent livelihood path.

**How to apply:** compare income and costs across amateur, pro, sponsor, manager, event-hosting, and streamer paths; keep purchases optional, ledger-backed, and compatible with existing saves.
