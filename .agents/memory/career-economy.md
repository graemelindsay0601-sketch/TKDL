---
name: Career economy scope
description: Product requirements for a balanced Career-wide economy and non-tour streamer livelihood.
---

Review the Career economy across all routes, not only tournament prize money or sponsorships. A player should be able to focus on streaming and make a viable living without pursuing professional tour progression.

Living costs must never be forced. Earnings should fund optional lifestyle and progression purchases, blending practical economic benefits with lifestyle milestones. Purchases are one-off and ledger-backed; practical upgrades must not change darts ability. DartCast studio investments improve future audience reach.

A player's follower count should unlock streamer opportunities, not determine sponsor rates on its own; price offers from recent average live viewers. League prizes should be funded by entry fees unless an explicit club or sponsor subsidy is shown. Add optional route-specific assets as money sinks, without upkeep.

A player at £0 needs a visible free/low-cost route to continue, but recovery income must still depend on results rather than guaranteed cash. Strong play should accelerate rankings, sponsorship and earnings beyond a baseline progression pace.

Optional DartCast creator series run across four quarterly chapters. Each chapter is earned by a real matching broadcast in that quarter; starting mid-season only includes the remaining quarters. Series choice never locks other formats or Career routes, and broadcasts are never started automatically.

**Why:** the user wants both a meaningful risk of failure and a fair chance to survive, continue their chosen route, and advance faster when playing well.

**How to apply:** compare weak, typical and strong outcomes across amateur, pro, sponsor, manager, event-hosting, and streamer paths. Surface affordable recovery options; keep costs optional where intended, purchases ledger-backed, and existing saves compatible.

**Why:** the user chose a four-quarter creator series and finale as the DartCast pacing; the series should add direction without forcing streaming or closing other routes.

**How to apply:** keep chapters tied to actual streamed formats, make missed quarters visible but non-punitive, and preserve mid-season entry and existing Career saves.

Series identity should shape sponsor-campaign format fit and mark compatible collaborators; an on-brand collaboration gives 35% more audience share. Campaign fees still use recent average live viewers.

**Why:** the user wants the DartCast series choice to have consequences beyond a checklist, without basing sponsor value on follower count alone.

**How to apply:** keep partnership offers relevant to the selected series, preserve the normal audience-size eligibility rules, and do not make series fit a route lock.

Travel planning is optional: retain the existing recommended route by default, and let a player choose a ground commute or flight with accommodation when the route supports it. Travel choice changes costs and sponsor coverage only, not sporting outcomes; old saves without a saved choice continue using the recommended route. For combined same-destination trips, the first explicit choice in event order governs the group.

**Why:** the user approved player-controlled route and stay choices, with the estimate including sponsor coverage, while existing Career saves must remain usable.

**How to apply:** carry the choice through affordability checks, reservations, and travel-week charges. Treat missing or invalid stored choices as the established recommended route.
