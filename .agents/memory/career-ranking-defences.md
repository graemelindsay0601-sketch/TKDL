---
name: Career ranking defences
description: Requirements for forecasting expiring ranking contributions without changing official standings.
---

Ranking defence forecasts are advisory. Calculate expiries and projected tables using the saved ranking window, publication clock, and ranking tie-break rules; never write forecast positions or values into official ranking state.

**Why:** the user approved a tracker to plan around expiring prize money, while official rankings must remain consequences of played and recorded events.

**How to apply:** label forecasts with their no-new-results assumption, show the specific contributions due to expire, and link only to future events whose ranking categories count toward the selected list.
