name: Career sponsor trade-offs
description: Product requirement for meaningful Career sponsor choices and working negotiations.
---

Career sponsorship should feel like one commercial world across long-term Career partnerships and DartCast campaign deals. Companies need distinct benefits, obligations, and negotiation behaviour—not only brand name and artwork. The user reports that current offers feel samey after limited play. Preserve active agreements and historical terms; migrate current saves additively, and allow counter-proposals on legacy offers where supported.

Offer one-off DartCast sponsor campaigns on a four-week cadence, not once per 52-week Career season.

**Why:** the user asked for sponsor choices with worthwhile pros and cons and said the proposal flow was not working.

**Why:** the user said a Career season is too long for the streamer route to feel realistic.

**How to apply:** share brand identities across long-term partnerships and one-off media campaigns while keeping their different deal mechanics distinct. Offer one campaign per four-week DartCast month, with at most one open campaign at a time. Differentiate actual terms and obligations, preserve historical snapshots, and test migration and proposal submission on legacy saves.
