---
name: Career sponsor trade-offs
description: Product requirement for meaningful Career sponsor choices and working negotiations.
---

Career sponsor offers should differ in meaningful benefits and costs, not only brand name and artwork. Players must be able to send counter-proposals from their existing saves.

**Why:** the user asked for sponsor choices with worthwhile pros and cons and said the proposal flow was not working.

**How to apply:** differentiate actual contract terms and obligations, preserve historical offer and contract snapshots, and test proposal submission through a legacy save as well as a newly generated offer.
