---
name: WDF ranking rules
description: Official WDF ranking and event-eligibility rules verified against the 2026 seniors criteria.
---

For the WDF Seniors World Ranking Systems Criteria, 50th revised edition (17 August 2026), rankings are points-based, not prize-money-based. Main rankings are rolling 52-week tables that count each player's best 10 points results; regional tables count the best 8 results in-region, and WDF majors do not count toward regional tables. Only singles tournaments or singles events are ranked. Current PDC Tour Card holders cannot enter WDF-ranked tournaments. Event points vary by grading and finishing position, with minimum field sizes. WDF has separate Youth (rev 19) and Under-23 (rev 5) criteria: both have Open/Girls rankings, their own age eligibility, the same point thresholds and 52-week best-10/best-8 tables, and exclude current PDC Tour Card holders.

Career has both official WDF fixtures and fictional WDF-inspired fixtures. Only grades verified against WDF sources are labeled official; fictional Career fixtures use explicit Career-authored tiers, never inferred official grades. Youth events are not included in the senior singles tables.

**Why:** Career's generic ranking-contribution pipeline sums A4 prize money, which is appropriate for PDC money-based Orders of Merit but not for WDF rankings. Fictional events need explicit game tiers without misrepresenting WDF sanctioning; team cups and youth events also have different eligibility from senior singles.

**How to apply:** Keep PDC prize-money lists separate. Use only sourced grades for official WDF fixtures; keep authored Career grades explicitly distinguishable. Any event added to WDF rankings needs points-valued contributions, 52-week best-N aggregation, regional eligibility, grade/finish points, minimum-field and completed-round checks, and eligibility checks. Keep Youth and Under-23 rankings separate from senior lists and from Career-authored season merit tables; do not award official WDF ranking credit for pairs events.
