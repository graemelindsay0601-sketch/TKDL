---
name: WDF ranking rules
description: Official WDF ranking and event-eligibility rules verified against the 2026 seniors criteria.
---

For the WDF Seniors World Ranking Systems Criteria, 50th revised edition (17 August 2026), rankings are points-based, not prize-money-based. Main rankings are rolling 52-week tables that count each player's best 10 points results; regional tables count the best 8 results in-region, and WDF majors do not count toward regional tables. Only singles tournaments or singles events are ranked. Current PDC Tour Card holders cannot enter WDF-ranked tournaments. Event points vary by grading and finishing position, with minimum field sizes.

**Why:** Career's generic ranking-contribution pipeline sums A4 prize money, which is appropriate for PDC money-based Orders of Merit but not for WDF rankings. Treating WDF team cups as singles ranking events is also incorrect.

**How to apply:** Keep PDC prize-money lists separate. Any accurate WDF ranking implementation needs points-valued contributions, 52-week best-N aggregation, regional eligibility, grading/finish points, and eligibility checks; WDF team cups must not enter singles ranking lists. Do not imply the WDF ranking model is accurate until those rules are implemented.
