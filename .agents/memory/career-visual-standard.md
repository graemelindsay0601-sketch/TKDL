---
name: Career visual and navigation standard
description: Product-wide visual and navigation expectations for TKDL Career screens.
---

Career mode should maintain a premium, visually distinctive presentation across all pages rather than relying on plain text blocks. Use TKDL's navy, red, and gold identity rather than a green-led palette. Screens should make key information scannable, keep labels and metrics inside their visual containers, and provide clear ways to move between related Career pages and return to the main Career areas. Weekly schedule entries should open the relevant event or activity. Career reviews and mockups must use the current premium shirt artwork, not legacy flat-vector shirts. A page redesign must cover its full content, not stop at the header: redesign the body sections and how their existing information is presented, while preserving real data and working actions. The user has clarified that text-heavy, boring pages are the core problem: typography and visual presentation need stronger variety, content must not look flat, and information should be made visually engaging instead of simply moved into more styled cards. Sponsor HQ specifically needs original imagery and distinctive sponsor/darts graphics rather than a uniform stack of panels, plus clearly raised surfaces, crisp bevels, and shadows so panels, metrics, and controls read as physical objects. The user expects visible, crisp 3D depth across the whole page—not only its hero or badge—with dimensional details that avoid fuzzy glow and keep text fitting cleanly. Crests and emblems must be fully visible and aligned at desktop and phone sizes, with all lettering contained inside the badge. Do not use real-life professional dart players in Career UI or Pro-Am content; use fictional players. For the Event Dossier review, they rejected cream/off-white and charcoal backgrounds and chose midnight navy with red and gold accents; do not present the rejected palettes as approved. Review this redesign one page at a time, wait for the user's sign-off before moving on, and do not push to main before all pages are approved. The user approved the Overview, Career Guide, and Calendar & Planning visual directions; use them as references for remaining areas.

**Why:** The user asked for a full visual and navigation overhaul because several Career pages, including Overview, My Career, and Club, felt plain and difficult to navigate, explicitly rejected a green-led palette, and corrected a review frame that still showed old standard shirts instead of the premium kits. They later clarified that header-only restyling, text-heavy content, and flat visual treatments do not meet their request; they expect the full page content and reading experience to be redesigned. On 2026-10-21, after seeing Sponsor HQ's first redesign, the user said it still felt dull and specifically asked for images/graphics and more visual distinction. After the imagery update, they liked the direction but again said the page did not feel 3D enough and asked for elements to stand out, confirming that dimensional relief needs to be obvious rather than implied by subtle shadows. They then pointed out that Representative communications & history still appeared untouched after the commitments area was revised, so this section also needs its own visual treatment. After the heading spacing was corrected, they approved the Sponsor HQ redesign; use its immersive imagery, distinct section treatments, and crisp raised depth as an accepted reference. They later noticed the bottom stroke of the gold “Q” in Headquarters looked blue, so preserve gold on the full glyph and its descender. They repeatedly emphasized that every Career area should stand out with crisp 3D depth, while rejecting fuzzy effects and text overflow; crests must also remain complete and aligned rather than cropped. They approved the raised Attention and Career navigation controls as better, confirming that crisp bevels and grounded depth fit the shared interface. They also approved the My Career Overview direction after a second pass increased its crisp dimensional depth. They explicitly said they do not want real-life professional players in the app. For Event Dossier concepts, cream/off-white and charcoal backgrounds were rejected; the user selected midnight navy with red and gold.

**How to apply:** When adding or revising Career UI, redesign content sections as well as page headers, check mobile and desktop layouts, navigation and return paths, and direct links from booked weekly items. On Sponsor HQ, give each section—including representative communications and history—its own visual treatment rather than only refining the selected block. Use the premium kit artwork on all Career mockups. Keep all additions compatible with existing Career saves. Present each page for individual approval before beginning the next; integrate/push only after the user approves the complete review set.
