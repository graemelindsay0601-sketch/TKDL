**Why:** The user wants visually distinctive Career pages because plain, text-heavy layouts felt dull. They repeatedly asked for crisp 3D depth and more engaging graphics, and specifically rejected blocky stacks and flat surfaces.

**How to apply:** Use TKDL's black, red, and gold identity, with smooth rounded raised surfaces and clear dimensional depth; avoid charcoal on dark Career pages, blocky panels, and fuzzy glow. Use varied premium typography, imagery/graphics, and full-page treatments while keeping labels readable and contained. Preserve real data, existing actions and Career-save compatibility; use fictional players and current premium shirt artwork. Review one page at a time, wait for approval, then integrate and push that completed step to `main` before moving on. Keep unapproved areas as previews. Provide clear in-Career navigation and ensure weekly schedule entries link to their event or activity.
