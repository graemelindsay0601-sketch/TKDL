name: Career money matches
description: Product requirement for making Career money matches genuinely playable.
---

Career money matches must launch the live scorer and be played by the user. Do not settle them by simulating the human player's match and immediately posting a result.

**Why:** The user explicitly said a simulated game is not what they asked for and that money matches should be playable.

**How to apply:** Keep the agreed stake and once-per-week rules, persist a resumable live dart log, record settlement exactly once after the scorer finishes, and only then post the stake transfer to the Career ledger.
