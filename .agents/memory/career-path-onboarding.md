---
name: Career path onboarding
description: Product intent for explaining playable Career routes when a new save begins.
---

New-save onboarding should explain the available Career routes, what each includes, and practical first steps. Routes can overlap and must not imply exclusivity or change eligibility unless the player explicitly chooses a mechanic that does so.

When a player explicitly chooses the DartCast route during save setup, persist it to that save, let them choose 1–3 stream nights per week, and reserve real available nights without starting streams automatically. Unplayed reservations should be released when the week passes; changing to an open route removes upcoming reservations.

The Career Goals page's DartCast creator focus is advisory: it changes recommendations, but does not activate the streaming route or reserve nights. Keep activation and scheduling explicit in Creator Studio.

**Why:** The user wants players to understand local/amateur, county and national team competition, Modus, streaming, Q-School, and ranking-event paths before starting. Career focus guides rather than locks; streamer activity is scheduled only at a player-chosen weekly frequency.

**How to apply:** Keep route descriptions accurate to current event rules and costs; preserve non-exclusive sporting eligibility; only create activity reservations when a player explicitly chooses that route.
