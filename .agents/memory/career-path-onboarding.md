---
name: Career path onboarding
description: Product intent for explaining playable Career routes when a new save begins.
---

New-save onboarding should explain the available Career routes, what each includes, and practical first steps. Routes can overlap and must not imply exclusivity or change eligibility unless the player explicitly chooses a mechanic that does so.

**Why:** The user wants players to understand local/amateur, county and national team competition, Modus, streaming, Q-School, and ranking-event paths before starting, rather than discover them across scattered screens.

**How to apply:** Keep route descriptions accurate to current event rules and costs; frame orientation as informational, and surface it again from Career Guide where useful.
