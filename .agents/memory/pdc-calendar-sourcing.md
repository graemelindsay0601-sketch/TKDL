The PDC fixture API endpoint has failed DNS resolution in this workspace, but `https://www.pdc.tv/sitemap.xml` remains accessible and exposes dated official news URLs that can confirm event ranges. For example, the official 2026 Women’s Series notice identifies events 21–24. The attached Darts-UK calendar is a useful discovery lead, not an authority: its 2027 event list and general series summaries conflict on some counts, and its stated revision date is later than the current project date.

**Why:** calendar completeness depends on distinguishing confirmed official events from stale or internally inconsistent secondary schedules.

**How to apply:** verify any PDC event count or fixture found in a secondary calendar against a current PDC news page or another official source before changing Career content; do not infer an annual count from an inaccessible API.
