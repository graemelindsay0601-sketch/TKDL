# TKDL Personalization System — Full Idea List

Card Clash dropped from this entirely, per your steer — everything below is standalone and lives in the new Store. Organized by area of the app each thing touches, so it's easier to see how big each category could get.

---

## 1. Profile Identity (your account page & player page)

- [ ] **Profile banners** — header background art behind your name/stats. Rotating set of themes (skyline, smoke & neon, chalkboard-pub, storm-on-the-oche, carbon weave).
- [ ] **Avatar frames** — decorative border around your profile picture, tiered by rarity (steel → gold → animated).
- [ ] **Animated name effects** — shimmer/pulse/gradient treatments on your display name, building on the name-colour styles that already exist.
- [ ] **Expanded profile icons** — more icon variety than what exists today (dart flights, mascots, seasonal one-offs).
- [ ] **Custom tagline** — a short personal line shown under your name on your profile.
- [ ] **Profile background texture/pattern** — a subtle full-page treatment behind your account content, separate from the banner.
- [ ] **Personal accent colour** — pick your own accent hue that tints buttons/highlights across your own profile page. This is the biggest one on the list — genuinely "make your corner of the app feel like yours" rather than a single decorative item, so worth flagging as its own mini-project rather than a quick add.

## 2. Leaderboard Presence

- [ ] **Purchasable titles/badges** — "Checkout King," "Iron Arm," etc. shown under your name on the leaderboard. Easy to keep expanding this set over time.
- [ ] **Row highlight glow** — a subtle personal colour glow on your own leaderboard row.
- [ ] **Flair icon** — a small icon next to your name (flame for a streak, trophy for a title-holder, etc.) — could double as an earned indicator rather than purchasable.
- [ ] **Rank-up celebration style** — a chosen animation/flourish that plays when you climb the table.

## 3. In-Match & Personal Screens (practice / self-play only — never a real match)

- [ ] **Result-screen accent theme** — recolours your own Practice/Master501/Tour result screens.
- [ ] **Victory celebration style** — pick between a few different win-screen effects (confetti, fireworks, spotlight).
- [ ] **Dartboard skin** — reskins the board visual on your own practice screens.
- [ ] **Personal scorer theme** — a colour scheme for your live scorer view during self-play.
- [ ] **Checkout flourish** — a small personalized visual moment when you hit a checkout in practice.

## 4. Trophy Case & Showcase (new concept — worth discussing on its own)

- [ ] **Trophy shelf** — pick up to 3 achievement badges to feature prominently at the top of your profile, instead of them being buried in the Achievements tab.
- [ ] **Featured stat spotlight** — choose one stat to headline on your profile (e.g. "Best checkout: 121").
- [ ] **Season recap card** — a shareable-looking summary card of a past season's highlights, with its own unlockable visual styles.
- [ ] **Milestone plaques** — cosmetic markers for things like 100 matches played, shown on your profile.

This whole category is really "let players curate what shows about them," which is a different kind of feature to the shop items above — more like a profile-editing screen than a purchase. Worth deciding if it's coin-gated at all or just a free customization layer that coins unlock *more options* for.

## 5. Your Player Card (the stat card shown on your player page — not a Card Clash collectible)

- [ ] **Card border/background styles** — cosmetic skins for your own stat card.
- [ ] **Card finish** — matte / foil-shimmer / holo treatment, purely visual.

## 6. Social (Messages feature)

- [ ] **Chat reactions** — quick reaction stickers in the Messages tab.
- [ ] **Message bubble colour** — a personal colour for your own sent messages.

## 7. Seasonal & Limited

- [ ] **Season-exclusive cosmetics** — banners/frames/titles that rotate with each TKDL season and stop being purchasable once it ends (scarcity, ties naturally into the season structure that already exists).
- [ ] **Tenure-based unlocks** — cosmetics that only become purchasable once you've been in the league a certain amount of time, rewarding long-standing members.

## 8. Small Utility (lowest priority, quick wins)

- [ ] **Name-colour change token** — a standalone re-roll outside the normal shop flow.
- [ ] **Extra saved dart-profile slot**, if there's a cap today.

---

## Scale check

This is genuinely a large feature area if most of this gets built — categories 1, 2, and 4 alone are enough for a proper "make this profile mine" system. My suggestion for how to approach it without it becoming one giant undertaking: treat each numbered category above as its own mini-milestone, and we pick which ones go first rather than trying to spec and build all eight at once. Categories 1–3 are the most natural next step since they extend shop patterns that already exist (Cosmetics shop, result screens); 4 (Trophy Case) is the most novel and probably deserves its own short design conversation once we get there, since it's more of an editing UI than a shop.

Let me know which categories you want prioritized (or if you want everything eventually, which order), and I'll update the visual mockup to match — bigger, with Card Clash removed.
